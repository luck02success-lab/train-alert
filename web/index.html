Built for one job.

RailWake isn't another booking or general train-tracking app. It exists to help you avoid missing the station where you need to get off.

How it works

Add your journey once. RailWake keeps the destination ETA updated and schedules reminders around the real journey.

1. Add your journey

Select your train, travel date and destination station.

2. We keep watch

RailWake adapts when the train is delayed, early or still waiting to depart.

3. We'll wake you

Get timely reminders before your destination, with at least 15 minutes between notifications.
